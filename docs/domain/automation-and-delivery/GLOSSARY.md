# Automation and Delivery Glossary

## Routine

A saved instruction that a Bot executes on a schedule or through an explicit trigger.

## Routine run

One execution of a routine, with its status, timing, result, and associated Bot work.

## Work item

A unit of Bot work that can be queued, resumed, or dispatched by the worker runtime.

## Bot task

A piece of work assigned to a Bot, including work created by a check-in or another product flow.

## Trigger

The event or schedule that starts a routine, responsibility, task, or delivery action.

## Delivery binding

The association between a conversation or Bot and an external delivery channel.

## Delivery inbox

Messages received from an external delivery channel and waiting to be processed.

## Delivery outbox

Messages that OpenBot has prepared to send through an external delivery channel.
