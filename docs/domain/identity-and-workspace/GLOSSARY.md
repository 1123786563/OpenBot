# Identity and Workspace Glossary

## Person

A human who uses OpenBot. A person may use Bots, answer approvals, manage their own settings, or administer an organization according to their role.

## Organization

The company or workspace that owns the team deployment, its people, Bots, policies, connections, and audit history.

## Bot

An AI coworker that a person can use or administer. A Bot has an identity, instructions, capabilities, a computer context, a work record, and a lifecycle.

## Personal edition

A single-person OpenBot deployment with one local user, one shared computer, and a Personal Assistant Bot.

## Team edition

An organization deployment where people, roles, Bot visibility, sharing, governance, and administrative controls are explicit.

## Bot visibility

The rule that determines which people can discover and use a Bot.

## Bot sharing

The act of assigning a Bot to people or groups so they can use it.

## Role

A person's authority within the workspace. The current product distinguishes administrator and regular user authority.
