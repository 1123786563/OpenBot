# Connections and Integrations Glossary

## Connection

An authorized link between OpenBot and an external service or account. A connection supplies access that a person or Bot may use under grants and policy.

## External account

An account belonging to a provider such as an email, calendar, CRM, or other connected service.

## External identity

The provider-side identity associated with an external account and its authorization lifecycle.

## Plugin

A packaged integration that contributes tools, connections, components, or skills to the workspace.

## MCP server

A service that publishes tools or resources through the Model Context Protocol.

## Tool catalogue

The set of tools and integrations available for discovery and authorization.

## Authorization

The process that proves a person or Bot may use an external account, connection, or integration.

## Delivery channel

An external surface through which OpenBot can receive or send a conversation, such as Slack, SMS, or another messaging system.
