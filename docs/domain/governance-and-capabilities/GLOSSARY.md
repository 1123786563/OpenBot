# Governance and Capabilities Glossary

## Capability

A product-level action or surface that a Bot or person may use, such as memory, skills, computer access, or a connection.

## Tool

An operation a Bot can call to affect the world or retrieve information.

## Skill

Reusable instructions or learned behavior that gives a Bot a repeatable way to perform work.

## Component

A registered interactive or executable capability that the workspace can expose to a Bot or person.

## Grant

An authorization that allows a particular Bot, person, or conversation to use a capability, tool, connection, or component.

## Approval

A human decision required before a protected action proceeds. An approval may come from a person, a team policy, or an already-issued conversation grant.

## Approval rule

A policy that determines which actions require approval and who may answer the request.

## Audit event

A durable record of a security-relevant or operational action, including who initiated it, what changed, and when it occurred.

## Policy

A rule that constrains an action or access path. Policies may govern tools, networks, computers, models, capabilities, or administrative changes.
