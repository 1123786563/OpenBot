### preload.ts warm-order fix — reasoning scrutinized, mechanism verified
- The change is exactly one reorder: `await import("react-dom/client")` now executes before the first `GlobalRegistrator.register()` (preload.ts:61 before preload.ts:75). The stated mechanism checks out at package level: react-dom's scheduler captures `scheduleTimeout`/`setImmediate` at module-evaluation time; happy-dom's `unregister` does not restore Node timers on `globalThis`, so evaluating react-dom after any register/unregister cycle freezes later timer-scheduled rerenders on a dead window. The symptom mapping (query cache has data, `findBy*` times out, act-flushed updates still work) is internally consistent.
- Bisect evidence exists and is real: the `.e2e/unit-h04-probe*` series (Oct 20 04:55–05:12) shows the claimed pass/fail pattern (probe/probe4/probe7 reproducing 3/3 and 1/1 failures on the domain suites; probeA/B/D/F green under fixed ordering), and the reports in `docs/verification/heroui-migration/h04/{native,probe}/` are genuine runner artifacts.
- Could warming earlier break anything? Server/worker tests: react-dom was already imported in every process before this change (inside the heroui-modal window); the reorder only moves evaluation to live Node globals — strictly safer, and preload.ts's header contract ("nothing else left holding browser globals") is preserved. The react-aria layout-effect warm (window 1) is unchanged and still happens after register. Driven-layer capture unaffected (verified: 20/20 driven rerun). Residual risks are honestly disclosed: (a) the Oct 19 23:03 anomaly (2 of 3 user-preferences passing before the fix) is unexplained — this review confirmed h04/native/report.json really shows 67/68 with only "shows loading…" failing, matching the report's account; (b) the heroui-spike TextArea import-order constraint cannot be regression-tested because the suite is excluded on both layers — correctly flagged for H02/H13 verify (F3).
- preload.ts is shared test infra established by H02/H03 — the reorder is disclosed with an explicit review request to those owners rather than silently absorbed. Process-correct.

The forwarded role is therefore dropped by the chain — the old `getByRole("combobox")` cannot match, and the ui/select.tsx:515-519 comment claiming the role "keeps the public contract" is factually wrong today (F4).

Not weakened.
